Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

PokeLorePanels Publisher ("the app") is a personal publishing tool operated by the owner of the PokéLorePanels channels. It is used only by that owner, on the owner's own accounts.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos and to read the channel's own video list and status (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).
  • TikTok: basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to send videos to the owner's TikTok account as drafts or posts.
  • Instagram (Meta): basic account info and permission to publish reels and read their insights.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and are used only to publish the owner's own videos and check that they were published. The app does not collect data about viewers or other users, does not sell or share any data, and does not use data for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google), in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and in Instagram under Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites. Revoking access stops the app; tokens are then deleted from the repository.
